and [upgrade matrix](../reports/2026-10-24_collector-hotfix-upgrade-matrix.md).

de-identified aggregate evidence in Git.

## Rollback

On failure, collect evidence, stop the candidate and prove its process/DB
ownership ended. Restore the previous EXEs from the verified backup and start
the previous service. Keep the DB, WAL, SHM, config, license and outbox from
the candidate run intact unless an operator-approved restore of the whole
consistent backup is necessary. Do not mix individual DB/WAL/SHM files across
snapshots. Schema-v1 checksum and packet/outbox format are unchanged, but the
old binary may rebuild indexes or need time to open. Verify counters and
handoff after rollback. If neither version can start, leave the single-instance
state stopped and escalate with the backup/evidence locations.

The automated 72-hour packaged soak, reboot/logout and forced-kill tests,
field DB startup preflight, rollback rehearsal and 7-day canary are still
`NOT_EXECUTED`. The first four block field cutover; the seven-day canary
blocks broad production promotion.
